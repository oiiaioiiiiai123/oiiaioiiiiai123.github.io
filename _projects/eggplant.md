  - title: Highlights and notes
    description: Highlight a sentence or select text within one paragraph. Add notes and revisit saved passages from Highlights.
  - title: Reading tools and history
    description: Adjust text size, line spacing and Light, Sepia or Dark themes. Reopen recent reading and view local reading statistics.

## About eggplant

eggplant brings English reading and vocabulary practice together on Android.

This is the first website showcase of the current **1.0 development build**. The app is still in development, and a public download is not available yet.
